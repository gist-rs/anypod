Noob Vibe Paper: CLaRa-Bridging Retrieval and Generation with Continuous Latent Reasoning

Got a powerful new approach to boost AI's knowledge accuracy? CLaRa is here to revolutionize how we think about retrieval and generation in language models.

🚀 **What’s new**: Introducing CLaRa (Continuous Latent Reasoning), a unified framework that combines retrieval and generation in one seamless process using shared continuous representations.
✨ **How it works**: The system uses a smart compression technique called SCP to create compact, semantically rich document vectors—making both retrieval and generation more efficient.
🤖 **Why it matters**: By enabling end-to-end training with joint optimization, CLaRa fixes major issues like context overflow and inefficient text handling, delivering state-of-the-art results on QA benchmarks.
