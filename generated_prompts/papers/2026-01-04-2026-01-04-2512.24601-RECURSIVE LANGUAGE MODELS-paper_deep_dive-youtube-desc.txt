🚀 How can we break the limits of language model context windows? Discover a groundbreaking new approach that lets AI process prompts up to 1 million tokens long!

✨ This video explores Recursive Language Models (RLMs), a powerful inference strategy that allows large language models to handle extremely long inputs by recursively breaking them down and processing them as part of an external environment.

✨ We compare RLMs against state-of-the-art models like GPT-5 and show how they maintain high performance on complex long-context tasks—way beyond normal model limits—while using similar or lower computational costs.

✨ Learn how this innovative method could revolutionize AI for long-horizon tasks, from complex reasoning to processing massive datasets, all without requiring changes to model architecture or training.
