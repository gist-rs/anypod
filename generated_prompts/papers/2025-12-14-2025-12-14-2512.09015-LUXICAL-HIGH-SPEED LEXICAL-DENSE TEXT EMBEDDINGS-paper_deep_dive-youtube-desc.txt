Noob Vibe Paper: LUXICAL HIGH-SPEED LEXICAL-DENSE TEXT EMBEDDINGS

Want to speed up large-scale text processing without sacrificing quality? Discover how Luxical brings the best of fast lexical models and powerful transformers together.

🚀 **High-Speed Processing**: Luxical delivers fast text embeddings by combining sparse TF-IDF features with a lightweight ReLU network, achieving up to 100x speedups over traditional neural models.

✨ **Flexible Embeddings**: Unlike classic classifiers, Luxical produces dense vector representations that support diverse tasks like clustering, retrieval, and classification—ideal for web-scale data curation.

🤖 **Efficient Training**: Using knowledge distillation, Luxical approximates large transformer models while maintaining high quality, making it a practical solution for AI research and production workloads.
