In his retrospective on 2025, Andrej Karpathy describes a year defined by paradigm shifts in the development and application of large language models. The introduction of Reinforcement Learning from Verifiable Rewards (RLVR) has allowed models to develop deep reasoning strategies, though this progress often results in jagged intelligence where AI excels at logic but remains prone to simple errors. Karpathy highlights the rise of sophisticated LLM applications like Cursor and Claude Code, which move beyond basic chat interfaces to act as integrated agents living directly on user hardware. This evolution has birthed "vibe coding," a movement where natural language enables anyone to generate functional, ephemeral software without traditional programming expertise. Ultimately, the text argues that LLMs are transitionary computing platforms moving toward a graphical user interface future that prioritizes visual information over raw text. Progress in the field remains rapid and open-ended, suggesting that the full societal impact of these "summoned ghosts" is only beginning to be realized.
